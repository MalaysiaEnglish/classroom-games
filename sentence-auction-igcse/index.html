💰 Sentence Auction

IGCSE English · Grammar & Accuracy · Y10 Term 2
How to play: Each team gets a budget of play-money. For every sentence shown, teams secretly decide (on paper or by whispering) how much of their budget they want to bid that the sentence is grammatically correct. The teacher enters each team's bid, then hits Reveal. If the sentence was correct, every team that bid on it doubles their bid (bid × 2 added to their budget). If it was incorrect, every team that bid on it loses that amount. Teams can also bid 0 to sit a round out safely. Richest team after all sentences wins!
