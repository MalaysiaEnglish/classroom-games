Horse Race

Sense of Place · Answer together · Reveal together · Gallop together
Cambridge Level 9 · Unit 6 · The City Sings, Grand Canyon & Chasm
How it works
  1. 1Every team answers the same question on a whiteboard or card.
  2. 2Tap each team's answer, then Reveal. Nobody knows who was right until then.
  3. 3All correct horses gallop at once. Two correct in a row = 2 spaces.
  4. 4Race cards every 4th or 5th question. Final stretch counts double. First across the line wins.
